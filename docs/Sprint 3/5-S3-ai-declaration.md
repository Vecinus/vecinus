## AI Use Declaration

Artificial intelligence tools were used during the development of this project as a support resource. Their use was supervised, reviewed, and adapted by the team at all times, and the final responsibility for the content and outcomes of the project remains entirely with the authors.

The following models and tools were used:

- Gemini 3.1: Used for brainstorming, code generation, debugging, documentation improvement, grammar correction, and conceptual explanations.
- Claude: Used for code generation, debugging, and code-style guidance.

## 1. Introduction

Throughout the development of this project, the team used artificial intelligence tools as a complementary resource to support both creative and technical tasks. These tools were never used as a substitute for human judgment, authorship, or validation.

All ideas, strategies, and final decisions included in this document were made by the team. The use of AI was limited strictly to assistance, support, and optimization under continuous human supervision.

## 2. Areas of Application

AI was integrated into the project in the following stages:

### A. Ideation and brainstorming

- Generating ideas for names, slogans, commercial descriptions, and visual styles.
- Supporting the structuring of content to be published on social media.

### B. Visual resource generation

- Prototyping advertising campaigns with sketch-style illustrations and simple figures used in the presentation of the project idea.
- Helping maintain a consistent visual style across published materials.

### C. Writing and summarization

- Drafting descriptions for social media posts and profile biographies.
- Improving style and tone to ensure the language was appropriate for each context, such as formal documents or public-facing content.

### D. Implementation support

- Resolving technical doubts and assisting with the structure or generation of code fragments.
- Supporting error identification and suggesting optimizations for the technical components developed by the team.
- Providing guidance on best practices for configuring tools, frameworks, or the overall project architecture.

## 3. Methodology

No AI-generated content was included in the final project without human review. The workflow followed by the team was:

1. A prompt was designed by defining the context and project objectives.
2. From the options generated by the AI, the one most aligned with the project vision was selected.
3. Texts were edited, designs were adjusted, and incoherent proposals were discarded to ensure quality and accuracy.

The team confirms that the final version of the project reflects its own decisions, criteria, and validation process.
